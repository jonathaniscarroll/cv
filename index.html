Major Projects

2026

Living Characters, browser-based AR world-building tool for NSCAD Art Camp: characters placed on a shared map, experienced in WebXR or camera-based AR with Three.js, dialogue trees, and Twee export.

2025–26

The Land Speaks, geolocated browser-based AR narrative about industrial pollution and autoimmune illness in Sydney, Nova Scotia, using narrative agents such as a toxic dust particle and a household curtain.

2025–26

Spatial Narrative, location-aware interactive fiction system (SugarCube 2, GPS, compass, dwell-triggered passages) with a reader and a PWA authoring tool.

2025–26

The Wrong John Carroll, cemetery archive and pallet altar project; Sourdough Synth, sourdough as bioelectric signal for solar-powered sound.

2019–present

VRIM (Virtual Reality Interactive Music), custom VR headset software and sound art composition tool for participatory electronic music performance in unconventional spaces such as forests, churches, galleries, and homes. Sounds made by the audience and the environs are sampled and processed into generative musical sequences, so each performance is unique to its space. Performed at the Art Gallery of Regina (2022) and developed at the Alchemy residency, Gibraltar Point Arts Centre (2025).

2021–2024

DETAIL: Digital Exhibition Toolkit, developer and curator of a software toolkit for digital art exhibitions using Unity3D. MacKenzie Art Gallery; Canada Council for the Arts Digital Strategy Fund.

2023

GAN of Living Skies, creator of a solar-powered AI landscape painting machine. Art Gallery of Regina; Saskatchewan Arts Council.

2022

Assassin’s Creed Art History, interactive video game exploring art history through archival research, part of a series of interactive “essays” that use assets from the Assassin’s Creed series as a lens on the representation of history in interactive media (captured in Unity as the animation Gameworkers Guildworkers). Art Gallery of Grande Prairie; Milan Machinima Festival (Critics Choice Award, 2024).

2022

Hypercity, GPS-based augmented reality exhibition across Toronto featuring six artists. Long Winter Festival; Canada Council Digital Now.

2021

Ender Gallery: A Minecraft Artist Residency, custom Minecraft mod and technical development for a four-part residency and exhibition series. MacKenzie Art Gallery; A MAZE. Berlin.

2021

Escape from Intern Purgatory, augmented reality app accompanying a graphic novel. Eyelevel Gallery; Nova Scotia Arts Council; Canada Council Digital Now.

2018–24

SpekWork Studio (with Cat Bluemke), games about labour. Gigco: Escape the Gig Economy, game and Unity-captured animation about the gig economy and automation; TAP Cop, browser-based idle game about defunding the police and refunding social programs; CO-OP Copter Scabs, game-jam helicopter simulator made during a refinery lockout in Regina, in which the player either flies scabs over the picket line or joins it.

2018

Resourced, virtual reality documentary with sound artist Ben McCarthy presenting the self-narrated stories of frontline workers (a street nurse, a harm reduction nurse, and social workers) affected by austerity, vicarious trauma, and mounting precarity, with interview content expressed through interactive metaphors, 3D-scanned animated figures, and spatial sound design. Mayworks Festival, Trinity Square Video, Toronto.

2021

Proof of Stake, an attempt to make art on the blockchain: 3D scans of property markers made in the woods of rural Nova Scotia, minted as interactive objects on the Tezos blockchain via hicetnunc.xyz.
