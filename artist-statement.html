Artist Statement

Jonathan Carroll · Interdisciplinary artist & creative technologist · Halifax, NS
← CVTeaching Philosophy

Jonathan Carroll is a multidisciplinary artist working primarily in interactive digital media, music, and performance art. Carroll makes art about labour, technology, and play, with a specific interest in exploring small-scale social histories about all three. He is currently working on an augmented reality project for his graduate studies at Nova Scotia College of Art and Design about the history of illness in his family due to industrial waste at the former site of the Sydney Steel Mill in Cape Breton, Nova Scotia. Collaboration is the primary form through which Carroll’s projects are created, whether with other artists in co-creation or with the audience through interactivity and participation.

Carroll attended OCAD from 2011 to 2016, where he formed a performance art collective called Tough Guy Mountain, whose work has been supported by the New Museum, the AGO, Rhizome, Eyelevel Gallery, Trinity Square Video, and Emmedia Arts. From 2018 to 2024 he worked collaboratively with Cat Bluemke as SpekWork Studio, making video games primarily about labour issues, showing at the Venice Architecture Biennale, Milan Machinima Festival, the Art Gallery of Regina, and Interaccess.

For the past ten years Carroll has been developing digital art presentation and exhibition applications for galleries and institutions, including Trinity Square Video, Long Winter, and for five years through a Digital Strategy Fund grant at the Mackenzie Art Gallery.
